# Pickup From Hand

Pickup From Hand is a Minecraft mod that lets players hand items directly to each other in a safer and more intentional way.

Instead of dropping an item on the ground, the giver holds the item out. The receiver must look back at the giver and right-click them to take it.

## Features

- Give the item stack currently held in your hand.
- The receiver must be within 2 blocks.
- The receiver hears a soft notification sound when an item is being offered.
- No chat messages or on-screen prompts are shown.
- The receiver must look at the giver and use an empty hand before taking the item.
- The receiver takes the whole held stack, not just one item.
- The item is placed directly into the empty hand used to take it.
- The give action is canceled automatically when the situation is no longer valid.
- While giving an item, the giver holds their hand forward with a visible animation.
- Both main hand and offhand are supported.

## How To Use

1. Hold the item you want to give.
2. Look at another player within 2 blocks.
3. Press `G` once to offer the item.
4. The other player looks back at you.
5. The other player right-clicks you with an empty hand to take the whole stack into that hand.

## Cancel Conditions

The offer is canceled automatically if:

- The giver changes the item in the offered hand.
- The offered hand becomes empty.
- The giver stops looking at the receiver.
- The receiver moves farther than 2 blocks away.
- A block gets between the giver and receiver.
- Either player enters spectator mode.
- Either player leaves the server.

## Notes

- This is a direct give system.
- The receiver does not need to press any keybind.
- Items are never dropped on the ground by this feature.
- The mod is designed to feel quiet and clean during normal gameplay.
